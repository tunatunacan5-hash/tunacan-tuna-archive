Tunacan Tuna

Tunacan Tuna is a Turkish culture writer, researcher, journalist, radio programmer, musician, performer, and actor based in Istanbul.

He graduated with honors from the Art and Cultural Management Department of Yıldız Technical University and develops research-based work on cultural heritage, museums, cultural memory, public history, visual culture, and the relationship between music and society. His work has appeared in international publications including DailyArt Magazine, Interlude.hk, and New Eastern Europe.

As an independent musician, he has built a substantial audience across digital platforms, with songs such as “Fena Karıştım,” “Suni Teneffüs,” and “Klarnet” reaching millions of views on YouTube. As a performer, he has appeared on stage hundreds of times, building a strong connection with live audiences through music and entertainment. He has also appeared in the cult Turkish comedy film Kutsal Damacana, with an IMDb credit under his artistic profile.

His writing combines cultural analysis, historical curiosity, and accessible storytelling. Alongside his artistic work, he contributes to international and Turkish media platforms with essays and articles on art, heritage, museums, cities, cultural identity, and collective memory. He has also contributed to curatorial and cultural projects at Uz Galeri.
