Tunacan Tuna

Tunacan Tuna is a Turkish culture writer, researcher, journalist, radio programmer, musician, and actor based in Istanbul.

He studied Art and Cultural Management at Yıldız Technical University and develops research-based work on cultural heritage, museums, cultural memory, public history, visual culture, and the relationship between music and society.

As an independent musician, he is known as a promising male vocalist whose songs have reached millions of views on YouTube, including “Fena Karıştım,” “Suni Teneffüs,” and “Klarnet.” He has also appeared in the cult Turkish comedy film Kutsal Damacana, with an IMDb credit under his artistic profile.

His writing combines cultural analysis, historical curiosity, and accessible storytelling. Alongside his music career, he contributes to international and Turkish media platforms with essays and articles on art, heritage, museums, cities, and cultural memory.
